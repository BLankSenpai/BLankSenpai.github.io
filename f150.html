Functional Overview

The 1995 F-150 powertrain featured roller-cam 302ci (5.0L) and 351W (5.8L) small-block V8s, or the durable 4.9L 300 Inline-6, all equipped with Speed-Density or Mass Air Flow (MAF) Multi-Port Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI).

1995 OBS Quirk & Notes

1995 California models and some 5.0L automatics received true Mass Air Flow (MAF) sequential EFI with dual oxygen sensors, while most 49-state models remained Speed Density with bank-fire injection. Look for the remote-mounted TFI ignition module on the driver side fender well!

Specs & Recommended Maintenance

  • Oil Capacity: 6.0 Quarts (10W-30 / 5W-30)
  • Firing Order (5.0L): 1-3-7-2-6-5-4-8 (HO Order)
  • Common Issue: PIP Stator Hall-Effect Failure
