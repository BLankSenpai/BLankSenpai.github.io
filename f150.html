System Description

The 1995 F-150 powertrain featured roller-cam 302ci (5.0L) and 351W (5.8L) small-block V8s, or the durable 4.9L 300 Inline-6, all equipped with Speed-Density or Mass Air Flow (MAF) Multi-Port Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI).

1992-1996 OBS Technical Quirk

1995 California models and some 5.0L automatics received true Mass Air Flow (MAF) sequential EFI with dual oxygen sensors, while most 49-state models remained Speed Density with bank-fire injection. Remote TFI ignition module sits on the driver inner fender well with an aluminum heatsink.
